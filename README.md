### 📖 The Engineering Focus
My work centers on building practical AI systems and bridging the gap between intelligent workflows and intuitive frontend design.

- **AI Integration & Agentic Systems:** Architecting end to end RAG pipelines and multi modal applications (LangGraph, FastEmbed, FAISS, pgvector, Llama Vision, Whisper) across builds like **[Along](https://github.com/AhmadCM01/Along)** (Meta Global AI Hackathon), **[Bilaad AI](https://github.com/AhmadCM01/Bilaad-AI)**, **[AbujaHommes AI](https://github.com/AhmadCM01/abujahommes-ai)**, and **[Afrilabs AI](https://github.com/AhmadCM01/Afrilabs-AI)**.
- **Frontend Architecture & Real Time Systems:** Building highly responsive, type safe client interfaces and distributed tools utilizing Next.js 14/15, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Zustand, Yjs CRDTs, and Redis across **[Boundless](https://github.com/AhmadCM01/Boundless)** and **[RateSentry](https://github.com/AhmadCM01/RateSentry)** (Vega IT Hackathon).
- **AI Evaluations, Safety & Product Growth:** Contributing as an AI Intern at **DecodeLabs** and Technology Intern at **AfriLabs**, while actively studying LLM evaluations and AI safety fundamentals (**Lens Academy**, **micro1 Evals Hackathon**, **Huawei HCIA AI v4**, **Claude Academy**) alongside 4+ years of Web3 community growth experience.
